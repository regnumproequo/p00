About this reconstruction · evidence, uncertainty & sources

A system assembled over centuries.

This is a cumulative schematic of ninth–thirteenth-century infrastructure, not one moment of “peak operation.” The main diagram simplifies relative placement. Canal connections, field parcels, contours and shoreline are illustrative; only the reservoir comparison has a metric scale.

The seasonal views and moving dashes explain roles and direction. They do not encode measured flow, historical water levels or crop yields. Reservoirs need not have operated simultaneously. Ritual, domestic and agricultural uses cannot be reduced to a single function.

Powerful, yet dependent on maintenance.

Research models suggest that extreme flooding could destabilize the network through erosion and sedimentation. This is one contribution to understanding Angkor’s transformation, not a single-cause explanation of its decline. Penny et al. (2018).
