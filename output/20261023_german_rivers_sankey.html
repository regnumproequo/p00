About this diagram & all river connections

Each upstream leaf contributes one schematic unit, which adds at junctions. Main-stem headwaters and unlisted tributaries contribute no units. A leaf marks the start of an illustrated branch, not a river's source. This selection covers six main systems; coastal rivers, canals, transfers, delta branches and underground connections are omitted.

Routes extend beyond Germany. The Rhein reaches the North Sea through its Dutch delta; the Oder reaches the Baltic Sea via the Stettiner Haff; the Donau continues to the Black Sea. Fulda and Werra join to form the Weser. German river names are used throughout.
