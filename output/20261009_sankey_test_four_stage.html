Transaction flows across four stages

Follow 246 synthetic transactions from ordering countries through ordering and receiving institutions to receiving countries.

Method: Transaction counts and country assignments are synthetic examples. Countries refer to the institutions. Bar heights and ribbon widths show transaction counts; bars share one colour per column. Each ribbon retains its receiving-country colour through all four stages.

Drag nodes to move the connected ribbons. Scroll horizontally on smaller screens to see all four stages.
