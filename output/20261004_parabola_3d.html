About the terrain & data sources

A 2 × 2 km summit-centred crop of Copernicus DEM GLO-30, sampled from a 1 arc-second digital surface model (AWS 2021 release; heights relative to EGM2008). Horizontal distances use a local latitude-adjusted projection; all three axes use kilometres without vertical exaggeration. Terrain ranges from 3,007 to 4,328 m in this display. The summit pin touches the highest displayed terrain sample (4,328 m) at 45.97674° N / 7.65674° E. Its label gives the independent summit reference of 4,478 m; terrain elevations are unchanged. A gridded model smooths sharp peaks; this is not a navigation map.
